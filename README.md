# P.S. — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 21, 2026_

P.S. ("the app", "we") is a daily affirmations app. This policy explains what
information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. We built
P.S. to work without an account: we never ask for your name, email or
password, and we do not sell your data.

## Information that stays on your device

These are stored only on your iPhone and never leave it:

- Your answers in the welcome questions (age range, sex, what you'd like more
  of, what gets in the way).
- Your favorite affirmations, chosen categories, theme, reminder times,
  streak and app language.
- Images you choose to save to your photo library. P.S. only asks for
  permission to **add** photos; it never reads your library.

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. No server sends them.

Deleting the app removes all of this information.

## Information processed by service providers

To run the app we use a small number of providers. Each one receives only
what it needs:

| Provider | Purpose | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Firebase Analytics** | Understand how the app is used so we can improve it (for example, which screens are opened or whether reminders are turned on) | App usage events, an app-instance identifier, device model, iOS version, app version, approximate country (derived from your IP address) |
| **RevenueCat** | Process and verify Premium purchases and restore them on your devices | An anonymous app user ID, your purchase history and subscription status, device and iOS version, approximate country |
| **Apple (App Store)** | Payments, subscriptions, refunds | Handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We never see your payment details |
| **Supabase** | Deliver the affirmation library | A standard request to download affirmations. No personal information is sent |

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not track you across
other companies' apps or websites, and we do not request access to the
advertising identifier.

Analytics events never include the answers to the welcome questions or any
text you type.

## Children

P.S. is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from them.

## Your choices

- **Notifications:** turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in iOS
  Settings → Notifications → P.S.
- **Photos:** you can change the permission any time in iOS Settings → P.S.
- **Subscriptions:** manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name →
  Subscriptions.
- **Your data:** because P.S. has no accounts, the information on your device
  is under your control and is erased when you delete the app. To ask us about
  data held by our providers (for example, to request deletion of analytics or
  purchase records linked to your device), contact us at the address below.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Brazil, you have the right
to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data.
We process analytics data based on our legitimate interest in improving the
app, and purchase data to perform our contract with you.

## Data retention

Analytics data is kept no longer than 14 months (the maximum Google Analytics
retention setting). Purchase records are kept for as
long as needed to provide Premium and to meet legal and tax obligations.

## Changes

We will update this page if the app's data practices change, and update the
date at the top.
